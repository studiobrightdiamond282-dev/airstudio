Dynamic Multi-Lens Engine

WebRTC Low-Latency Core

Live in three steps

No drivers, no IP addresses, no app-store detours.

STEP 01

Open the Studio

Launch the Studio on your PC. It generates a 6-character pairing code and a QR code instantly.

STEP 02

Scan with your phone

Point your phone camera at the QR code. The camera page opens pre-paired — approve camera access and you're connected peer-to-peer.

STEP 03

Direct your shot

Switch lenses, dial in exposure, blur the background, roll the teleprompter — then send the clean output to OBS, Zoom or Teams.
