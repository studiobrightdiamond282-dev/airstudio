AirStudio

Turn this phone into a wireless studio camera. Enter the pairing code shown in the Studio on your PC — the video link is peer-to-peer and encrypted.

🎙️ Microphone is OFF by default. Starting the camera requests video only — tap the 🎙️ button to enable phone audio for the program feed.

iPhone/iPad: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install AirStudio as an app.

Tip: keep this tab in the foreground while streaming. Use the 🌙 battery-saver once you're framed up.
